Save PBC Prayer to Your Phone

Select your phone, then find the browser you're using. These instructions save the PBC Prayer logo to your home screen.

Which browser are you using? Tap a browser name to see its steps.

Samsung Internet
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in Samsung Internet (the purple planet icon).
  2. Tap the menu ☰, usually at the bottom right.
  3. Choose Add page to.
  4. Tap Home screen.
  5. Tap Add, then confirm if asked.

If your version shows a + button in the address bar, you can tap it and choose Home screen instead.

Google Chrome
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮ near the address bar.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, Install app, or Install (the label varies).
  4. Confirm with Add or Install.

The prayer request form now opens on Providence’s website, avoiding the previous Google account sign-in issue.

Mozilla Firefox
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in Firefox.
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮ by the address bar.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen or Install.
  4. If asked, name the shortcut PBC Prayer and tap Add. Confirm again if prompted.

Make sure you select Add to Home Screen (your phone), not Add to shortcuts (the Firefox start page).

Brave Browser
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in Brave.
  2. Tap the three dots ⋮ to open the menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Add or Install to confirm.

Depending on Brave's version, you may be offered either a website shortcut or an installed web app. Both provide home-screen access. If Brave says “This app cannot be installed”, close the message, reload this page, and try Add to Home screen again. If it still fails, open this page in Samsung Internet and use its Home screen option instead.

Microsoft Edge
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in Edge.
  2. Tap the three dots … menu, often at the bottom.
  3. Look for Add to phone. You may need to swipe across the menu options.
  4. Tap Add to phone or Install, then confirm.

Some Edge versions call this Add to Home screen.

Opera / Other Android Browsers
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in your preferred browser.
  2. Open the browser menu or its + button near the address bar.
  3. Look for Add to Home screen, Add to…, or Install.
  4. Choose Home screen, then confirm with Add or Install.

Menu names vary by browser. If you cannot find a home-screen option, copy this page's link and open it in Samsung Internet, Chrome, or Firefox.

Safari (recommended)
  1. Open this PBC Prayer page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the upward arrow). On some versions, first tap the page menu.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If shown, leave Open as Web App enabled.
  5. Keep the name PBC Prayer and tap Add.

If Add to Home Screen is missing, scroll to the bottom of the Share sheet, tap Edit Actions, and add it.

Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge, or another iPhone browser
  1. On this page, open your browser's Share menu and choose Copy Link, or copy the address from the address bar.
  2. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  3. Paste the copied link into Safari's address bar and open it.
  4. Tap Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  5. Name it PBC Prayer and tap Add.

Safari offers the most consistent home-screen setup across iPhone and iPad versions.

After adding it, look for the PBC Prayer icon with the Providence church logo. Tap it, then press Submit Prayer Request to submit a prayer request, or choose another church link.

Having trouble? Open the prayer form using another browser. If the form does not open, check your internet connection, refresh the page, or contact the church for help.

There is no app-store download. An internet connection is required to submit prayer requests.

Instructions may look a little different depending on your browser version.
